# Code of Conduct

Regent is a small project about trust. The standard is simple.

**Expected:** be direct and specific; critique code and ideas, not people;
assume competence and good faith; accept that maintainers make the final call
on scope and will explain why.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption of issues and
pull requests.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit, remove, or lock contributions and may
ban contributors who violate this standard. Serious violations get one
warning at most. Report concerns privately through GitHub's report feature or
to a maintainer; reports are handled confidentially.

This applies in the repository, its issue tracker, discussions, and any
project-run chat.
